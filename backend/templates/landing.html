Platform Architecture

Six Pillars of Complete Identity Management

Built from the ground up for zero errors, high security, and high production speed.

Visual Drag-and-Drop Designer

Build templates directly in your browser. Configure photos, dynamic text tags, barcodes, QR codes, watermarks, and emblems with millimeter-accurate guides.

Flutter Teacher Mobile App

Equip teachers with a native Android & iOS mobile app to capture verified student portraits directly with their phone cameras, eliminating studio queues.

Anti-Tamper Public QR Verification

Every card carries a unique cryptographic QR token. Anyone scanning it with an ordinary smartphone camera opens an instant, official verification page.

Excel Import & Validation Engine

Upload school-wide spreadsheets in seconds. Automatic class-section mapping, roll-number collision checks, and clean error reporting prevent data corruption.

Commercial Batch Print Engine

One-click batch PDF generation with industry-standard 8-up or 10-up card layouts, vector typography, and precise cutting crop marks for dye-sublimation PVC presses.

Multi-Tenant Data Isolation

Military-grade role segregation guarantees that students, teachers, and client organizations are strictly isolated. No cross-tenant data bleed ever.

Mobile Companion App

Put Student Photo Capture Directly in the Teacher's Hands

Say goodbye to lost digital camera SD cards, chaotic USB sticks, and mismatched student names. Our Flutter companion app connects teachers directly to their assigned classroom roster.

Instant Camera Snapping & Auto-Crop

Teachers tap a student's name, take a portrait photo, and upload it directly to the verification queue in 3 seconds.

Real-Time Verification Queue

School administrators review submitted photos in the web portal before cards enter the print queue, preventing blurry prints.

Optional Bypass Mode

Organizations that do not use mobile devices can disable the app toggle with 1 click. Enrolled students are then automatically verified for printing.
